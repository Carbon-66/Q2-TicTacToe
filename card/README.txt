TIC-TAC-TOE for the Shanling Q2 (Q2 Pod)
=========================================

Three in a row wins. Play the Q2, or a friend on the same Q2.

INSTALL
1. Copy the .tictactoe folder to the root of the card. On a Mac, press Cmd-Shift-. in Finder to
   see dot-folders.
2. Eject the card and open Library -> Games -> Tic-Tac-Toe.

MODES
  Play vs Q2     You are X, the Q2 is O. Choose its level on the menu (Centre on "Q2 level"):
                   Easy    misses wins and blocks, and slips a lot
                   Normal  plays well, but now and then misses a block or slips
                   Hard    nearly perfect: a slip is rare, so watch for it and pounce
                 The Q2 knows the perfect move but doesn't always take it, so it can be beaten
                 on every level, not just drawn.
  Play with a Friend
                 X and O take turns on the one Q2.
Who starts swaps every round.

CONTROLS
  Wheel          move the highlight round the empty squares (or the menu)
  Centre         play in the highlighted square; after a round, start the next
  Touch          tap a square to play there; tap a menu line to pick it
  Return         back to the menu
  Quit           the X in an O at the bottom of the menu: leave the game
  Hold Return    leave the game from anywhere (Q2 Pod's own)
The music keeps playing: Play/Pause and the side buttons stay the player's.

FILES
  q2-tictactoe     the game
  tictactoe.sav    the Q2's level, and your record against it at each level
  tictactoe.log    what the game did, for when something goes wrong
